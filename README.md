# Customer Shopping Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Customer Shopping Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze customer shopping behavior, sales performance, product trends, and revenue patterns.

The dashboard provides a clear view of important business metrics and helps identify trends based on customer demographics, product categories, locations, payment methods, and seasons.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall customer shopping performance
* Understand revenue and transaction trends
* Identify top-selling products
* Compare revenue across product categories
* Analyze customer distribution by age
* Compare revenue across different locations and seasons
* Understand customer preferences based on payment method, gender, and category

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

* **Clothing** generated the highest category revenue at approximately **$104K**.
* **Accessories** generated approximately **$74K** in revenue.
* **Footwear** generated approximately **$36K**.
* **Sunglasses** was the top-selling item with approximately **10K** sales.
* Revenue was analyzed across multiple locations to identify differences in customer spending.
* Seasonal revenue was compared using an interactive donut chart.
* Customer counts were analyzed across different age groups.
* Interactive filters allow users to explore data by **Payment Method, Gender, Category, and Season**.
